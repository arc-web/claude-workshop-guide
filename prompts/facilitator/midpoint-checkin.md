# Midpoint Check-in

At the halfway point, each pod sends one person up for a 2-minute pulse check. Not a full demo - just a quick status.

---

## Share this with all pods 5 minutes before the check-in

```
Midpoint check-in in 5 minutes. Pick one person from your pod to speak.

Paste this into your terminal to prep:

"Help me prepare a 2-minute midpoint update for our pod.

Here's where we're at:
[paste your notes.md]

Write a quick script covering:
- What we built so far
- What was easy
- What blocked us
- What we're doing next

Keep it honest and specific. No corporate speak."
```

---

## Format (for the facilitator to run)

1. Call each pod up in order (30 seconds transition between)
2. Timer visible - 2 minutes max per pod
3. After each pod speaks:
   - "Anyone else hit the same blocker? Raise your hand."
   - "Anyone solve it? Share how - one sentence."
   - Move on.
4. Total time: ~15 minutes for 4 pods

**If a pod goes over 2 minutes:** they're explaining too much. The point is the blocker - say it fast so the room can help.
